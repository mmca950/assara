# Legal notes

This is not legal advice. It explains the rules the method is built around, so that a library stays on
the right side of copyright and platform terms. You remain responsible for what you add to your library and
for what you publish from it.

## Copyright and personal-use licences

Most books, courses and subscriptions are protected by copyright, and many are sold under a licence for
**personal use** only. Assara is designed for that situation:

- What you take from a source is **general knowledge**: concepts, methods, facts, figures, with a citation.
  Ideas and facts are generally not protected; their expression (wording, structure, examples, slides,
  illustrations) is.
- `original/`, `raw/` and `units/` contain the protected expression itself. They stay **private**: never
  published, shared, uploaded to a public repository, or sent to anyone. The `.gitignore` of a new library
  excludes them.
- Notes are your own rewriting, but they follow the source closely and cite its pages. Keep them private
  too (a private repository is fine).
- **Every public output is rewritten in your own words**: no sentence, slide, named example or passage
  copied as is. Short quotations, where your law allows them, must be attributed and limited to what your
  point needs. The "For <project>" sections of the syntheses are written this way from the start.
- Studies and authors are named so that you can go back to the primary source and cite it properly.

## Platform terms

- **YouTube**: the terms of service restrict downloading content without permission. Assara fetches
  subtitles (and, if none exist, audio for a local transcript) of a single video for your private study.
  Check that this use is acceptable for the videos you add, prefer videos whose owner allows it, and never
  republish the video, its audio or its transcript. Playlists and channels are refused by design.
- **Instagram**: the terms forbid automated collection at scale. Assara reads a single public post
  that you give it, without logging in, for private study. Do not use it to crawl accounts, and do not
  republish images or captions.
- **Web pages**: respect the site's terms and `robots.txt`; a page behind a login is recorded as unreadable,
  never worked around.

## No DRM circumvention

Assara does not remove, bypass or weaken any technical protection measure (DRM, encrypted streams,
licence servers), and contributions that add such a capability will not be accepted. Circumventing these
measures is illegal in many countries even for content you have paid for. Only add files you can open
without such tools: DRM-free books, your own recordings, material whose licence allows a copy.

## Your own outputs

AI-assisted notes can contain errors. The method keeps citations, "To verify" marks and flagged garbled
passages so that you can check before you publish. Check figures in the original before using them in
public.
