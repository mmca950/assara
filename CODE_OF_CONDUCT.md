# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that address applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other channel.

## In short

- We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body
  size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
  level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
  colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Expected: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, constructive feedback, accepting
  responsibility for mistakes, focusing on what is best for the community.
- Not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
  political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without
  permission, and any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate.
- Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, suspend
  or ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the project's
GitHub page (or by a private security advisory if no other channel is listed). Every report is reviewed and
handled in confidence.
